Who this policy covers

This policy describes cookies and similar browser-storage technologies for the KATUNI landing page at katuni.kileleni.net, operated by Kileleni Labs. Contact kileleni6@gmail.com with questions.

What cookies do

A cookie is a small value a website or its infrastructure can store in your browser and receive on later requests. Browser technologies such as local storage can also retain information on a device.

What the current page uses

The current KATUNI landing-page code does not set its own cookies, use local storage or load advertising and audience-tracking scripts. The interactive walkthrough keeps its temporary stage only in the current page’s memory. Reloading the page resets it.

The website is intended for delivery and protection through Cloudflare. Security features enabled for the domain may issue essential technical cookies. The exact cookies depend on the configuration and whether a security challenge is triggered.

Your choices

You can inspect, block or delete cookies using your browser’s privacy settings. Blocking cookies required for a security challenge may affect access. Browser settings and private-browsing modes vary, so consult your browser’s help information.

The current page has no optional advertising or audience-measurement cookie categories to enable. If we add optional technologies that require consent under applicable law, we will explain them and obtain the required choice before using them.

Android app storage

Android recordings, scripts and preferences are app data managed through the app and Android settings. This browser-cookie policy applies to the website. See the Privacy Policy for the app’s local storage, dictation and optional transcription.

Changes and contact

We will revise this policy when the site’s technologies or configuration materially change. For questions, email kileleni6@gmail.com.
