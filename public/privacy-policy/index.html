Privacy Policy

How KATUNI handles website visits, recordings and optional processing.

Effective November 25, 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceCookie Policy

Who operates KATUNI

Kileleni Labs operates KATUNI and this website at katuni.kileleni.net. You can contact us about privacy at kileleni6@gmail.com.

This policy covers this landing page and the Android preview offered here. Processing by a speech provider or transcription server you choose is also governed by that provider’s own privacy terms.

Information processed when you visit the website

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes technical request information to deliver and protect the website. This can include your IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, time of access, and security events. Depending on the service configuration, technical request logs may be available to us.

The landing page presents product information, an interactive sample and downloadable files. Its code does not request microphone access, create an account, collect payment information, or send the sample walkthrough text to an AI provider. The current site has no advertising or audience-tracking scripts. Cloudflare security features may use essential cookies as explained in our Cookie Policy.

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to reply and handle your request. Please send only the information needed for your enquiry.

Recordings and text in the Android preview

The Android preview stores recordings, drafts, bookmarks, scripts and transcripts in local app storage on your device. Microphone permission is used when you choose to record or dictate. Basic recording and replay operate locally.

You control local recordings through the app. You can export a copy, move an item to Trash, restore it, or permanently delete it. Uninstalling the app or resetting the device can remove locally stored data. Copies that you export, share or back up are managed by the destination you select.

We do not receive your locally stored recordings simply because you use the recorder. The preview does not include automatic account-based backup or an analytics SDK.

Dictation and optional transcription

Dictation uses the speech recognition service available on your Android device. Where the device supports an on-device recognizer, processing may happen locally. A system speech provider may instead process audio online, depending on the provider, language and settings. Review that provider’s privacy information.

For optional transcription of a saved recording, the preview lets you configure an HTTPS server and confirm before uploading the selected audio. The destination server receives the audio and the technical request information needed to respond. Its operator and any API provider it uses determine their processing, retention and deletion practices. The returned transcript is saved in the app.

Choose a server and provider you trust. If we introduce a Kileleni-operated transcription or AI service, we will disclose the providers, data sent, retention and choices for that service before making it available.

Why information is used

We use information we receive to provide the website and support you request, protect the service, investigate faults and misuse, and meet legal obligations. Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, these activities rely on providing requested services, our legitimate interests in operating and securing them, applicable legal duties, or consent when consent is required.

The website does not use personal information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Who information may be shared with

Cloudflare provides website delivery and security. Our email provider processes support correspondence. A provider may process information outside your country as part of operating its services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect rights or address security incidents.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. When you choose to share audio or use an external speech or transcription service, that destination receives the content you send.

Read Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for information about its processing. International-transfer safeguards depend on the service and applicable law. Contact us if you need details about transfers of information we control.

Retention and security

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it, clear app data or uninstall, subject to your device’s behavior. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle the enquiry and any related security, legal or dispute matters, then reviewed for deletion. Hosting and security records follow the applicable provider configuration and retention practices. We have not set a universal fixed retention period for those provider-managed records.

We use HTTPS for the website and confirmed transcription requests. Your device protects local app storage through its operating-system controls. Transmission, storage and recovery depend on the services and devices involved, so export important recordings and use appropriate device security.

Your choices and rights

You can manage microphone permission in Android settings, choose whether to use dictation or transcription, control your local recordings, and manage cookies in your browser. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you.

Depending on the laws that apply, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. We respond within the period required by applicable law and may need information to verify the request. We cannot retrieve or delete a recording stored only on your device or in an independent service you selected.

Children and policy updates

KATUNI is a general productivity service. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We may update this policy when the service or its processing changes. The date at the top identifies the current version. We will provide additional notice where required for material changes.

Contact

For privacy enquiries and requests, email kileleni6@gmail.com and identify KATUNI in your message.
